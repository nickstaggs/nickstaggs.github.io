**How Big Things Get Done: The Surprising Factors That Determine the Fate of Every Project, from Home Renovations to Space Exploration, and Everything in Between**\
*Bent Flyvbjerg and Dan Gardner*\

1. Think slow, act fast
    - Most project don't take long enough to plan and rush the implementation
2. Think from right to left
    - what is the goal?
    - Why are we doing this?
3. Pixar Planning
    - iteration and experimentation
    - experiri -> experiment and experience 
    - "Planning is iteration and learning before you deliver at full scale"
4. Experience
    - use tried and true methods and technologies
    - rely on leaders that have done similar projects
5. Your project is not unique
    - use reference class forecasting to estimate time and budget
    - your project probably fits with in some kind of reference class from which you collect data from to make estimates
    - risk mitigation 
6. There is a survivorship bias for just doing things
    - you only hear about the projects that succeeded by just doing it
7. You need a team that's all in
8. Modularity or what's your lego
    - Find the smallest piece that can be broken off to be repeated over and over again
        - A room in a school
        - A floor of a building
        - The foundation, tower, head and blades of a wind turbine
